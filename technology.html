ROBOT INTELLIGENCE

What lets a robot decide, not just move.

Autonomy is the layer that turns a mobile machine into a robot capable of operating in a live factory.

Localization

Determining the robot’s precise position on the facility map in real time.

Navigation

Moving safely between defined points across a shared, active floor.

Path Planning

Calculating efficient routes and adjusting them as conditions change.

Obstacle Avoidance

Detecting and safely responding to people, equipment and unexpected obstructions.

ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES

How we approach the stack.

Built for Integration

Every layer is designed to connect with existing machines, controllers and factory systems rather than replace them outright.

Modular by Design

Hardware, firmware and software components are structured to evolve independently as requirements change.

Engineered for Reliability

Real-time systems and industrial communication are treated as first-class engineering requirements, not afterthoughts.
