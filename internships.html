WHY INTERN WITH IYONEX

Learning grounded in real engineering work.

Hands-On Engineering

Work on practical engineering problems rather than learning only through theory.

Robotics Exposure

Understand robotics systems, mobile robots, sensors, controllers and autonomous technologies.
