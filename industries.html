INDUSTRIES

Automation across industries.

Autonomous material movement applies wherever things need to move — reliably and repeatedly — across a facility.

Automotive

Component movement, line-side delivery and intra-factory logistics across assembly operations.

Manufacturing

Raw material, work-in-progress and finished-goods transportation between production stages.

Electronics

Controlled movement of components and materials between sensitive process steps.

Warehousing

Autonomous transportation and workflow automation across storage and dispatch zones.

Heavy Industry

Industrial material handling and repetitive transportation in demanding environments.

Custom Industrial Operations

Automation solutions designed around unique operational requirements outside a standard category.

HOW WE EVALUATE FIT

Every facility is different.

Rather than fitting your operation to a fixed product, we start by understanding your specific material flow.

Workflow Assessment

We study how material currently moves through your facility, including bottlenecks and manual steps.

Environment Mapping

Floor layout, traffic patterns and existing infrastructure are reviewed before any deployment plan is proposed.

Phased Deployment

Solutions are typically introduced on a defined route or process first, then expanded once validated.

Not sure where your industry fits?

Tell us about your operation and we’ll help identify where automation would create the most value.
