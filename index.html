THE PROBLEM

Factories are automated. Material movement still isn’t.

Modern factories run advanced machines, but material still moves between them by forklift, fixed conveyor and manual handling.

Manual Dependency

Repetitive material transportation still depends heavily on human effort.

Inefficient Movement

Unoptimized material flow creates delays between production processes.

Safety Challenges

Manual transportation and forklift movement can create unnecessary operational risks.

Limited Scalability

Traditional systems can be difficult and expensive to adapt as factory requirements change.

Iyonex is building the bridge between robotics and real-world factory operations.

FLAGSHIP SOLUTION

Move materials. Not people.

The Iyonex Autonomous Material Movement Platform automates repetitive transportation across factories and warehouses — sensing, planning and coordinating without fixed rails or wires.
